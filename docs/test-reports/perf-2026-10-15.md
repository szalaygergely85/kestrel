## Findings and next 3 JS cuts

1. **`engine/render/gpu/MeshBuffers.js:getVoxel` -> `buildMeshTriVertexData` (13.6-16.3 %, 1.3-1.6 ms/frame, #1 in every pose).** Called from `passRaster._hookFn` and `_decorDraw` every few frames, not once: the cache check `existing.mesh === mesh` (and `meshVersion`) misses, so the vertex/index/AO buffers are rebuilt and re-uploaded (also feeds `writeBuffer` 5-6 % and probably part of `writeTexture`). Hypothesis: lazyMesh/LOD re-creates MeshData objects for the same id, or meshVersion bumps. Cut: key the cache on id+meshVersion only (or find who re-creates the mesh), keep one upload per mesh. Likely -1.2 ms.
2. **`engine/mesh/DrawList.js:addMeshStructures` (4.2-7.1 %) + `resetDrawItem` (2.8-7.1 %)** = fable S3: the linear scan of all placed structures per frame and per-item reset. Cut: iterate `placementGrid` cell members (same push order, draw-list hash must stay identical at the 5 poses) and pool/skip reset for unchanged items. Likely -0.5..-1.0 ms.
3. **Voxel pose: `engine/voxel/voxelPose.js:computeVoxelPose` + `matMul3` + `buildRot` (6.5-10 % together)** = the per-frame `voxelPool.collect` over beast/fauna slots. Cut: skip pose recompute for dormant/off-screen/unchanged slots (BEAST-POOL-a/b/c already planned) and cache `buildRot` per yaw. Likely -0.3..-0.5 ms.

Also visible but smaller: `writeTexture` 9.7-10.4 % (one per-frame texture upload; find which, possibly the uniform/lighting LUT or particle layer; candidate cut 4), terrain heights `heightFn` (world_geo.js) + `recipeHeight` (overworld_far.js) 4-4.5 % together (terrain pushes, `pushTerrainItem` 2.8 % at roadBend). Sprite sun visibility and beast sim do not appear in the top 10, so S4 (sprite amortisation) is not the next cut.
