# PO paper play-through - Chapter 1 "Beyond the Wall" (2026-10-10)

(PC-B PO opus, owner request 2026-10-10). Read-only review of branch `pc-b` at e1b779c8. No browser run: this is a paper walk of
content + wiring against D-058 / D-062 / D-064 and the owner's play-test complaints. Line numbers are from that commit.

Sources: content/quests/{m1,tower.blade,burl.boars,areas}.json, content/dialogue/bear.{dialogue,barks}.json, design/models/notes.js,
design/models/title.js (storyHints), content/levels/tower.level.json (interactables), content/worlds/world_m1.world.json
(bear/boars/endMarker/relayBend/triggers), game/js/main.js (markers, compass, CH1 mount, toasts), game/js/questRelay.js,
game/js/quest/{burlEscort,relayWake,crystal,chapterCard,doorToggle}.js, game/js/quest/sim/{questBook,questMarkers}.js, game/js/ui/questLog.js.

Root cause behind most owner complaints: **two quest systems run side by side.** The linear main quest `m1` (10 steps, HUD line,
section toasts, `'!'` objective markers) still carries the whole chapter, and the giver quests (`tower.blade`, `burl.boars`) were
layered on top. They disagree on titles, completion moments and markers.

Edge cases checked:
- **Skip the note:** dead end at Burl (row 6).
- **Kill the boars first:** row 7.
- **Relay first:** row 13. Without the crystal, E gives "Cold stone. Your hands are not enough." (fine).
- **Leave by the door before climbing:** the `towerDoor` fact latches, so `leave` completes instantly when the sword is taken at the summit. You get the toast "Leave the Tower" on the roof and `'?'` over Burl. Minor and acceptable.
- **Death mid-escort:** `burl.phase`/`burl.wp` are saved and `load()` resumes the walk. If the reload predates `s.burl.follow`, Burl replays follow.1 (entry `q.burl.boars.done`). Recoverable, no soft-lock found. Needs one browser check (respawn point before any stone is woken).
- **Old saves:** migrateM1Ch1 + migrateQuestSave cover lantern/fen/boars. There is a gap at old-`sword` saves (PO-CH1-09).
