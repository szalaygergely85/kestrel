## Where a village could use them (suggestion)

1. Houses: Wall_Plaster_* / Wall_UnevenBrick_* + Corner_* on Floor_WoodDark, topped with Roof_RoundTiles_NxM (pick by footprint) or Roof_Wooden_2x1 for sheds; Overhang_* for a second-floor jetty.
2. Doors and windows: Door_N_Round/Flat inside DoorFrame_*, Window_*_Flat/Round with WindowShutters_* (open/closed variants give cheap life) and Window_Roof_*.
3. Streets and yards: Prop_WoodenFence_* around gardens and the Burl clearing, Prop_MetalFence_* at a bigger house, Prop_Wagon + Prop_Crate at the market corner.
4. Approach: Stairs_Exterior_* with Platform for hillside houses, Balcony_* on the tallest building, Prop_Chimney* and Prop_Vine* to break up the silhouettes.
5. Ruined/abandoned look (fits the story): drop Roof pieces, add Prop_Vine*, Prop_Brick1-4 rubble and HoleCover_* as broken floors; keep tri budget by merging walls per house at import.
