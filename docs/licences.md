## Publisher evidence follow-up — 2026-10-07

**Ruins.** [Makovice's Ancient Ruins pack](https://makovice.itch.io/ancient-ruins-assset-pack) offers `RuinsGLB.zip` and describes matching ruin pieces, destructible walls and skeletons. This is a strong candidate, not a verified archive match. All 56 local GLBs expose only Blender exporter/version metadata, with no author or licence field. The page labels its licence CC0, permits commercial projects and optional credit, but says “Sell or redistribute these assets as standalone files” and “Include them in another asset pack” are prohibited. That conflicts with the unrestricted distribution described by the [CC0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Keep the discrepancy unresolved; do not classify these files as verified unrestricted CC0.

**Cozy Nature.** The [FREE page](https://thomasgamboa.itch.io/cozy-nature-free-voxel-style-modular-enviorement-asset-pack-and-textures) still has no explicit licence in the retrieved text. A VoxelNest reply in the linked [FULL page's comments](https://thomasgamboa.itch.io/cozy-nature-full-voxel-style-modular-enviorement-asset-pack-and-textures#comments) permits personal/commercial projects without attribution and prohibits resale, redistribution or repackaging of raw assets. It permits sharing projects using them. The reply occurs on the FULL product page; applicability to the tracked FREE files is unconfirmed. It is evidence of FULL terms, not a CC0 grant for FREE.

**StickyBizcuit.** Rechecked the [publisher page](https://stickybizcuit.itch.io/voxel-asset-pack-decorations-and-environments), including its broad free-use comment. Neither that text nor the retained readme explicitly resolves commercial use or public raw/converted redistribution. The retained hidden-username requirement remains recorded above. No publisher was contacted.
