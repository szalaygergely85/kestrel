# World map - geography, regions, level bands, biomes (designer, 2026-10-11, PROPOSED)

Owner asks: "the map about the map ... sea and mountains etc." + layout notes of 2026-10-11 (enclosed world, sealed gate, river through the middle, sand beach, biomes, level bands). Data: `design/levels/world_geo.js` (`ASSETS.levels.world_geo`, not loaded by the game yet). Preview: `design/preview/world-map.html` (proposed vs today's recipe, light slider, level tint, hover readout). Frame = `overworld_far.js` (x east, y south, z relative to the tower floor; lowlands ~ -68 m). Names marked (P) are proposed; canon names: Ferrum, the Wall, the Crown, the Low Wards, the Kestrel, the Hollow Watchtower, Outwall (working), Bend/Ford Relay, the Signal Source. No dungeon (D-065): mines/caves are shallow open sites.

## 1. Layout and borders (why this way round)
- **The river is the spine**: today's `x_r(y)` (x ~1020-1250) is kept unchanged and extended: source = Highspring tarn (P) (1193,130) in the Whitecombs, through Glasswater lake (1235,505), past the North Bank, the ford (1028,1068), the Reedmire fen, out through an estuary at (1180,1725).
- **North = the Whitecombs (P)**: snowy range along y < 330, peaks to ~+330 m; seen from the meadow 700-900 m away (~17 deg up).
- **West = the West Fells (P)** (today's `recipe.ridge` grown into mountains) with **the Sealed Gate (P)** at (240,840): a fortified pass (curtain wall + 2 towers + barred gate) at the end of the Old West Road. Visible, reachable, locked; the key is the last quest (Part II after release). Beyond: not built.
- **South = the sea**: sea cliffs in the west (Fells, Signal Head), sand beaches + dunes from the estuary east (the Long Strand (P)), cliffs again under the Wall.
- **East = Ferrum's Wall (canon)** at x ~1985 from the Whitecombs to the sea: the Wall Law closes the land bridge. Bounds stop at x 1880 (killing ground, ballistae). Ferrum (tiers, the Crown, amber lights) sits beyond the map as a horizon backdrop (centre 2450,1000). This fits the canon: the Kestrel flew west over the Wall, was shot down and crashed at the tower 490 m inside.
- The pencil line (relays) runs from the Wall west-south-west to the signal tower; the Kestrel fall line runs from the Wall to the tower (brass scraps).

## 3. Relay route (the pencil line, D-060 stages)
Wall (1985,1004) -> Ash Mile relay stump (P) (1760,1012, dead, out of bounds) -> **Hollow Watchtower** (1496.5,1024.5, st 0) -> **Bend Relay** (1262,1033, st 1) -> **Ford Relay** (992,1079, st 2) -> Outwall relay (912,952, st 3) -> ws_signalNorth (695,1090) -> **Signal tower** on Signal Hill (713.8,1232.1, st 5). Stage 4 (North Bank) is a side branch up the north track to Glasswater. Optional side relay: Mire relay (1060,1470).
Roads (world_geo.roads): the existing road (unchanged), Old West Road (crossroads -> ws_signalNorth -> Sealed Gate), north track (crossroads -> Outwall -> Glasswater), mill track (-> North Bank -> mill -> quarry), south track (meadow -> Downs -> Gullhaven), coast path, fells path, head path.

## 4. What the player sees from the meadow / breach
West: the road falling to the river valley, forest belts, the Thornwood dark beyond, the signal tower's teal light on Signal Hill (800 m WSW) and, behind it, the grey Fells with the gate towers as a notch. South-west/south: the Reedmire flats and a glint of sea 650-750 m away (8-9 deg below eye level from the summit). North: the white Whitecombs filling the skyline, Old Pale highest. East (back): the Ash Mile, then the Wall's line of towers 490 m away and Ferrum's amber tiers above it.

## 5. Terrain recipe changes (implement in overworld_far.js from world_geo.js; reference maths = `world_geo.util.geoHeight`)
0. **Coordinates / shapes (architecture 38.42)**: engine coords, x east, y SOUTH, metres (all numbers here and in the preview). Numeric data in `world_geo.js`: `regions[].r` rects [x0,y0,x1,y1], river = today's `x_r(y)` (+ `river.bedNorth/bedSouth`, `estuaryWiden`), `coast.pts` closed polygon + per-segment `kinds`, `ranges[].pts` ridge polylines + `w`, `peaks`, `lake`/`marsh` rotated ellipses, `gate.pass` ellipse, `roads[].pts`, `sites`, `wall`. The world stays 2048 m (fits the 4096 m limit; the far east beyond x 2048 is backdrop only).
1. **Protect mask**: all new layers x `mask(x,y)`; 0 inside rect x 704..1728, y 832..1344 (38.42) and disc r 160 at the signal tower, smoothstep to 1 over 150 m. Today's heights AND forest types there stay bit-identical (so the Thornwood's dense core and the Ash Mile's dead wood start west of x 704 / east of x 1728; preview: `mask` in the hover readout, "protect zone" toggle).
2. **South tilt**: -26 m x smoothstep(y 1250 -> 1780).
3. **Mountain ranges**: per spine polyline `h*exp(-(d/w)^2)` + ridged fbm crest `amp*rn^2*exp(-(d/0.7w)^2)` (rn = 1-|2 fbm-1|, 4 oct). Whitecombs h 230 w 260 scale 170 amp 90, river gorge keep 0.25 within 90 m of the river; West Fells h 170 w 190 scale 150 amp 60. Peaks: 5 gaussians (Old Pale +120 r 140 ...). Snow line z +110, rock +40, alpine +5.
4. **Gate pass**: ellipse (250,840) rx 230 ry 55, flatten to floor -30 for e < 0.6, blend to e = 1.
5. **River bed** (only where mask > 0, within 40 m of x_r): min(h, profile): north y 100:+45, 300:-20, 420:-56, 600:-60, 760:-72; south y 1300:-92, 1500:-95, 1730:-99. Water half-width 10 -> 45 m across the estuary (y 1450..1730).
6. **Lake Glasswater**: ellipse (1235,505) rx 165 ry 90 rot 0.2, surface -58, bowl depth 10, shore band 0.3 flattened to surface +0.8. Needs a water region (like quietPond) at z -58.
7. **Reedmire**: ellipse (1110,1560) rx 230 ry 140 rot 0.55, flatten to sea +0.8 +- 0.3 noise (blend 0.4); pools where fbm(x/40) > 0.6.
8. **Coast**: sea polygon `coast.pts` (22 points), sea level **-96**. Sea: seabed sea - min(14, 1.5 + 0.08 d). Beach segments: blend to sea +1.2 over 90 m, dunes +3.5 m around 55 m inland. Cliff segments: land within 120 m raised to sea +22, 12 m wall. Land never below sea +0.6.
9. **The Wall + Ferrum**: not terrain. Wall x_w(y) = 1985 + 14 sin(y/310), y 380..1705, 24 m, towers every 160 m; Ferrum as a far silhouette (like farTower): tiers r 430/300/170 at z +40/+110/+190, amber emissive cells. Bounds east limit x 1880.
10. **Forest density by region**: `regions[].forest.thr` replaces the global 0.55 outside the mask (0.34 Thornwood ... 0.8 Downs); `kind` picks the tree mix (section 6).

`*` = animal not built yet (voxel, on all fours, D-016 / owner rule); boars, deer, crows exist (D-053). Biome edges use the noise field itself (no hard lines); region thresholds change over the 150 m mask ramp.

**Perf budget (for engine planning):** dense forest 1.4-1.8 trees/100 m2 = 14-18k trees/km2; the forward half of a 384 m tree band is ~0.23 km2 -> **~4k tree instances** visible: needs GPU instancing + LOD1 (all tree meshes have `_LOD1`) beyond ~64 m and the far grid beyond 384 m. Ground scatter at 60-130 per 100 m2 (flower/grass fields) = 0.6-1.3 M/km2: keep it to a **48 m near radius** (~4.6k m2 forward -> **~6k instances**), clumps billboard/fade past 32 m, Petal_* only within 20 m. Suggested cap: 4k trees + 12k scatter instances per frame; the open flower/grass biomes are the worst case, not the forests.
