**Architect tech notes (2026-09-24).**
1. **Root cause (confirmed by a Node probe, float64 and float32 alike, so NOT a float32/`<=` grazing issue):** the **GPU is right, the CPU is wrong.** Traced ray (col 111, row 13 at 9x16 px cells): the ray crosses the 1.4 m solid cell (tower local x 16) from h 1.420 down to 1.378, so it hits the wall top at t 2.699 (row 13 lies in the cap's projected range (12.57, 13.49]). `castColumn` draws that cap row (`castPlane(..., GK_TOP)` after the solid-cell wall face), but then sets `openBottom = min(openBottom, wallRowStart - 1)`, which leaves the **cap rows open**. 1. **Binding is a component, not a type:** `components.voxel = { model, anim, t, frame, loop, speed, playing }` (same shape as `sprite`); `EntityHandle.play/stop` and `stepAnimations` act on `sprite ?? voxel` (`animComponent(e)`). No clips needed for burner/relay/lamp/heaps in M1.
