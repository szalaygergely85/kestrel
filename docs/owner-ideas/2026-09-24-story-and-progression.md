# Owner idea: story hook + progression (2026-09-24)

Raw owner idea, to be shaped by the writer (story), the PO (GDD/backlog) and the manager (scope/decisions). Not canon until recorded in docs/game-design.md / docs/decisions.md.

## Story hook
- The game **opens with an animation of the crash** (the balloon *Kestrel* going down into the tower).
- Backstory: years ago **someone close to the hero vanished from the city** (sister, love, father... the relation doesn't matter yet). Everyone says they are dead.
- Years later the hero **receives the SOS signal**, recognises it, and goes after it ("he goes and jumps"), which leads to the crash.
- Fits D-011 amendment 2 (hero Wick from machine-only Ferrum, SOS, crash into the tower, magic real outside). The writer proposes how the vanished person and the signal connect.

## Progression (no XP)
- **No experience points / no grinding** ("I don't like that farming").
- **Items have levels**: weapons, clothes/armour, shields, etc. Power comes from gear.
- **Magic** is gained at **beacons and wells** and by **finishing quests**, which unlock a **small skill tree**: fireball, freeze, lightning, and **one big spell**. Keep it small.
- **Arrows** (bow) as well.

## World and systems
- Quests, **trading**, **crafting**.
- **Animals** around, **monsters**, **plants**.
- Biomes: **huge forests, desert, snow, mountains, wind**.
