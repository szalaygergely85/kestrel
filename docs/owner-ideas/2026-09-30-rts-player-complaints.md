# RTS player complaints -> opportunities (owner, 2026-09-30)

Owner input for the RTS design (D-032), pasted in chat from the owner's research into community discussions. Backlog row: **OWN-REQ-012**. Input for the PO/manager when the RTS GDD is written after an RTS-01 GO. It doesn't change the spike.

| Player complaint | Opportunity for us |
|---|---|
| Too much focus on competitive multiplayer | Strong single-player campaigns |
| High APM and constant multitasking | Deeper strategic decisions with less frantic clicking |
| Campaigns are short or treated as secondary | Narrative-driven missions and replayability |
| Few meaningful co-op experiences | Co-op campaigns and PvE progression |
| Limited logistics and supply mechanics | More believable economies and battlefield systems |
| Too many similar military settings | Unusual themes and new kinds of conflict |
| AI opponents feel predictable or unfair | Adaptive, reactive AI |

## Notes for the design pass (main session, not decisions)
- Several rows already fit D-032: single-player first, skirmish/campaign vs AI, and a deterministic sim that keeps lockstep co-op possible later.
- "Less frantic clicking" matters for the readability spike too: slower, more deliberate play suits a 400x150 ASCII view where units are only a few glyphs tall.
- "Unusual themes" lines up with the parked setting decision (D-032: "setting decided later"). The parked canon (D-011, steampunk accents, Ferrum) is one candidate. The writer could propose alternatives after the spike.
- "Logistics and supply" and "adaptive AI" are the biggest scope items. Each needs its own epic and architect notes (supply = sim systems; adaptive AI = game/js/rts AI on the deterministic sim, RE-14).
