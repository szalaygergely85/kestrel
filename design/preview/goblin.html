Kestrel : GOB-D1 - goblin raiders of the west bank (D-072)

design/models/voxel_goblin.js: goblin (melee + thrower: nail-studded club left, javelin right, two spare javelins on the back), goblinTorch (the same rig, a lit pitch torch raised in the left hand: emissive flame voxels = EMIS-01b derived light, preset torchSconce), goblinSpear (the thrown javelin). 1.05 m, hunched, long arms, big swept ears, hooked nose, sunken ember eyes. Every clip plays in every view; the clip buttons pick it (the thrower in the 240x90 shots always plays throw). Day = palette.timeOfDay.morning, dusk = .dusk, night = .night + the torch light at the engine's derived centroid (pm.emissiveLight, posed by the root part like voxelPool.offerEmissive), flickering; no shadow rays. Rendering: engine packVoxelModel / computeVoxelPose, the preview CPU march, palette.util.shade, approximate edge pass. Needs http: python -m http.server from the repo root, open /design/preview/goblin.html.
