Kestrel : OB-153 - Eurasian lynx (lynx), day and dusk

design/models/voxel_lynx.js: a 1.1 m lynx on four long legs, rump higher than the shoulders. Short round head, forward amber eyes, black ear tufts, cream facial ruff with black bars, spotted tawny coat, cream belly, big pale paws, short tail with a black tip. 12 parts (knee rig). Pick a clip: idle, walk (cat walk), run (bounding gallop; charge = run), alert, windup (pounce crouch + butt wiggle), attack (pounce + bite, snap at 320 ms), eat (crouched at the kill), hurt, hitAdd, flinch, die (rolls onto its side), dead, sink. Day = palette.timeOfDay.morning, dusk = .dusk; custom = the sliders. The light azimuth slider moves the sun in every row. Rendering: engine packVoxelModel / computeVoxelPose, the preview CPU march, palette.util.shade, approximate edge pass (as crocodile.html). The preview plays each clip alone with authored legs (the game adds the leg solve and cross-fades). Needs http: python -m http.server from the repo root, open /design/preview/lynx.html.
