Kestrel : grey wolf (WOLF-D1, design v1.60) - model key wolf

design/models/voxel_wolf.js: 10 x 33 x 22 voxels @ 0.05 m (1.35 m nose to rump + 0.45 m tail, 0.8 m at the withers, 0.3 m wide), 8 parts (body, head, jaw, 4 legs, tail), meshOnly, no flash shell. Rendering = the engine's packVoxelModel / computeVoxelPose, the preview CPU voxel march (lib/voxelMarch.js), the v1 reference shader (palette.util.shade) and an approximate edge pass. Tris from the engine mesher. The boar (boarPlaceholder) is loaded for the budget checks and the side-by-side read test. Needs http: python -m http.server from the repo root, open /design/preview/wolf.html.
