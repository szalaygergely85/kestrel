Kestrel : ENV-03-DESIGN : biome atmosphere (fog, haze, colour grade)

Proposal for the owner, nothing in the game changes. Each biome has 2 atmospheres (A / B), each shown at day / dusk / night (D-064) on the same small sample scene: sky band (palette skyCyan* / skyEve* / skyNight*, horizon tinted by the biome haze), far hills (~1.5 km), near hills (~450 m), trees (12-140 m) and the biome ground (the ground look you picked on ground-richness.html, default A). An atmosphere = fog colour + density, horizon haze, and a 3-way grade (lift / gamma / gain + saturation) per time of day. In game the look cross-fades by the biome under the camera (ENV-03: >= 1.5 s or >= 20 m, no pops). Data: design/look/biomeLook.js. Untick "grade" or "fog" to see what each part does.
