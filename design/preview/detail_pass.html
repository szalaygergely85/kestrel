2. Why today collapses to one glyph (numbers, ambient only)

Every texel class of the materials test_room uses, shaded by the v1 reference at 3 m with ambient 0.12. The texture only changes brightness, so all the classes land on ramp index 1 or 2. That gives . or ,, in colors about 10/255 apart.

3. Material close-ups: near / mid / far, dark / lit

Each swatch is a flat patch facing the camera, sampled at the game's 160 x 60 projection scale for that distance (near 2 m, mid 6 m, far 14 m). Dark = ambient 0.12 only. Lit = torch 1.0 (r 6 m) 1.2 m in front of the patch. The bottom row of a wall swatch is 4 cm above the floor (moss, soot and seam AO). "Today" uses the nearest v1 material (brick and the timber ceiling have none, so today's test_room ceiling shows stone).

4. Distance: texture LOD and fog

A stone wall, then a floor, seen from 1 m (left) to 40 m (right). Today, the texture fades from 6 to 16 m and the glyphs go to blank in fog (12 to 60 m). Proposed: near/mid/far glyph sets with a hashed dither (mid from 6 m, far from 13 m). Joints stop when they would be denser than 1 per ~2 cells. Fog starts at 6 m, full at 36 m, and turns into a lighter blue . : haze instead of black.

5. Glyph vocabulary (sets)

Each set is a list of density levels (the darkest is left). A group like ,:; is a set of alternates with equal density: brightness picks the level, and a world-anchored hash picks the alternate. So a wall at one brightness still shows three or more glyphs, and they don't shimmer when you move. Joints are not in any set. They are drawn as oriented lines _ - / \ | that follow the line's on-screen direction.

6. Edge pass rules

Runs over the finished cell grid. It needs depth, the plane id and the surface kind per cell. Tick "show edge map" above to color every cell a rule touched. One cell wide, never a full black outline (style guide 5.9). The rule gains brighten or darken the cell's own color.
