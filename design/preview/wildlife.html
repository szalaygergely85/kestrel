Kestrel : EP-WILD step 1 preview - wild rabbit + deer (design v1.53)

design/models/voxel_wildlife.js: rabbit (European wild rabbit, 0.42 m, cellM 0.02, 12 x 21 x 16) and deer (red / roe hind, 1.6 m long, 1.0 m at the withers, cellM 0.05, 12 x 32 x 33) + deerBuck (small roe-buck antlers). 8 parts each, meshOnly, ellipse-slice bodies (no flat boxes), natural coat variation. Four-legged in every clip; the rabbit's alert sits up on its haunches (owner-approved natural alert pose). State -> clip map, gait speeds and flee distances: ASSETS.wildlifeFx. Rendering = the engine's packVoxelModel / computeVoxelPose, the preview CPU march (lib/voxelMarch.js), the v1 shader (palette.util.shade) and an approximate edge pass, same as voxel_bear.html. Tri counts from engine/mesh/voxelMesh.js. Needs http: python -m http.server from the repo root, open /design/preview/wildlife.html. Kinematics checks (feet planted, nothing sinks, nose on the grass): node tools/voxel-wildlife.test.mjs.
