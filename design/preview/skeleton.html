Kestrel : OB-191 - risen skeletons (warrior + archer)

design/models/voxel_skeleton.js: skeleton (warrior: rusty short sword right, broken round shield on the left forearm) and skeletonArcher (old self bow with bending limbs and a string that meets the nock, rotten back quiver, nocked arrow while drawing). 1.8 m human skeletons from 5 cm voxels: open ribcage, two-bone forearms and shins, deep dark eye sockets, yellowed grime, grave moss. The clip buttons pick the clip for each model (warrior-only / archer-only clips fall back to idle on the other one). die collapses into a bone pile, rise pulls the skeleton up out of the pile (1.2 s), draw / shoot are the archer's attack. Day = palette.timeOfDay.morning, dusk = .dusk, night = .night (kinds.json: skeletons walk at night). Needs http: python -m http.server from the repo root, open /design/preview/skeleton.html.
