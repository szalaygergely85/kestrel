1. Scene vignette : torch, sun shaft, lantern, moss, soot, sky

Not a render of the game, a mood target. Left wall = north side (moss band near the floor), right = brazier (scorched stone, cold ash on the floor), sun patch on the floor with dust motes in the shaft, lantern on its hook glinting brass. The broken wall top shows the sky. Uses the time-of-day selector.

2. Materials under light

Each swatch = 2.4 m x 2.5 m of wall, 1 texel per cell (about the look at 3 m). Bottom row = floor level (for moss / soot bands). Columns: Dark = ambient 0.12 only | Torch = brazier 1.0, r 6 m, 1.2 m in front | Sun = directional, N.L 0.8, left part in shadow | Lantern = 0.8, r 5 m, 1.4 m in front | Interactive = the controls above (distance slider applies fog + texture fade here only). The sky row shows the four times of day instead.

3. Glyph ramps

Index 0 is always space (darkest). Top line = the ramp string; bottom = brightness 0 to 1 mapped through util.rampGlyph (cutoff 0.03, gamma 0.85, 1 is brightest), tinted with the material color.

4. Distance fog and texture fade

Interior rule: stone lit at torch level, seen at 0 to 60 m. Texture contrast fades between 6 and 16 m (anti-shimmer), fog starts at 12 m and is full at 60 m (color fog, glyphs fade to space). Second strip = far overworld rule (US-016): 50 m to 1500 m, fog color itself goes from fogFarNear to fogFar (= horizon).

5. Sky by time of day

Elevation 0 (bottom) to 50 deg (top). Emissive, not fogged. M1 uses morning only; the rest are ready for M2 day/night.

6. Color language and UI

Warm = light, safety, the player's goal. Cool = shadow, distance, the Dim. Gold/brass = "you can use this". See style-guide.md.
