1. First person: the spell glove, casting, the fireball in flight and the burst

A stone hall: floor 1.6 m below the eye, side walls 2.5 m left/right, the end wall at the "wall at" distance (the crosshair target). Lights: the light sliders (ambient + one directional), the spellEmber glow at the coal (scaled by glow per state), the moving fireballLight / fireballLightBig, the fireballFlash / Big (9 steps, (1 - age/9)^2). The ball starts at castOffset (eye frame, hand sign) and flies to the crosshair hit at 16 m/s (tap) / 12 m/s (charged); the trail is burstAt(fireTrail, 1 or 2) every 2nd step; the burst = fireballBlast sprite + fireballBurst + fireballSmoke (+ fireballCinders on a floor hit) + the flash + the camera kick (within 6 m). The glove is drawn last, mirrored for the right hand exactly like 37.8a (S = diag(-1,1,1)).

4. The pack screen (uiStyle.inventory on the fixed 160x60 UI layer)

Click the screen to focus it. I / Esc open/close, W/A/S/D or arrows select (W from the top grid row goes to the hands strip), Q / LMB = left hand, E / RMB = right hand, Enter / U = use (meat +10 HP, "Not hurt" at full HP), LMB / RMB / Enter on a hand slot empties it. Mouse hover selects. The scene grid toggle only changes the scene behind (plate + dim map to more scene cells); the UI cells are identical at every grid size (US-091b AC).
