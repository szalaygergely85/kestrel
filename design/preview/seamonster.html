Kestrel : the sea monster past the Long Strand (seaMonster) - circle, rise, strike

design/models/voxel_seamonster.js: a 7.3 m sea serpent, cellM 0.1, 7 parts (body, neck1, neck2, head, jaw, tail1, tail2). The anchor is the WATERLINE (entity z = surfaceZ in every state); the water plane here is z = 0. circle = only the crusted back hump, the spiny dorsal fin and now and then the tail fin break the surface (optional 38.23 dark tint k 0.6); rise = head and neck come up (~1.2 s, held); strike = recoil, 2.5 m lunge with a 45 deg gape, SNAP at 250 ms. "water" off shows the whole body. The in-game rows are 240x90 crops seen from a swimmer's eye (0.4 m over the water, level). Light azimuth slider moves the sun. Rendering: engine packVoxelModel / computeVoxelPose, the preview CPU march, palette.util.shade, approximate edge pass (as black-bear.html). Needs http: python -m http.server from the repo root, open /design/preview/seamonster.html.
