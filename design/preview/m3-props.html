1. The old sword pell (orbit + 6 yaws)

The watch garrison's sword post, there long before Wick: squared stone socket with moss, iron wedges, a silver-grey oak post, a straw bundle lashed with three rope bands (two old sword cuts on the front, one on the back, tufts hanging under it), a crossbar with a broken west end and a linen rag on the other, a dented rusty iron kettle helm. flash = the white shell (4 slabs, 1 voxel proud, stored 64 voxels under the floor at rest) for 2 x 50 ms = 6 steps while the head twists 4 -> 8 deg on the post; wobble = 8 -> -5 -> 2 -> 0 deg, 530 ms. hit = flash -> wobble -> idle, with the sword's -*- spark and the optional straw puff.

2. Pell in-game size (engine projection, flagstone floor, ivy wall 1.6 m behind)

Eye 1.6 m. Typical views: 5 m (from the room / the burner side) and 1.4 m (sword reach 1.6 m: where the player stands to hit it). The view-angle slider walks round it (0 = straight at the cut front, which faces west toward the sword nook in the level).

3. HP and MP pickups (billboards)

pickupHp = a small stoppered flask sealed with red wax, crimson liquid glowing faintly, a twist of herb tied at the neck (field medicine, nothing magic). pickupMp = a leaning splinter of crystal with a cold blue light breathing inside (1.7 s) and motes drifting off it - no bottle, no teal: Wick has no word for it. Liquid / glow keys are emissive, so both read on sunlit grass and in the dark tower. Hover + bob + the last-3-s blink are view-only (pickupStyle).

4. Vitals HUD (160x60 UI layer over the scene) + hurt edge + death card

Left: scene 160x60, right: scene 240x90 - the UI layer is the same 160x60 grid on both, so the bars keep their size (UI cell 6x9 px here = 12x18 px at 1920x1080). Buttons drive a presentation-only stand-in for vitals (hp 30, mp 20). Low HP (<= 25 %) pulses at 1 Hz; a hit plays the damage chip on the lost cells and the 0.15 s ragged hurtEdge; a short spendMana blinks the MP bar twice. Death: eye sink 0.8 s, fade 1.5 s (approximated here by darkening; the game uses the end-card LUT), then the writer line types on and [E] Wake again appears.
