1. Valley layout (top-down, north up)

Borders: north + west = mountains (unclimbable above the slope limit, collider-backed), south = sea (shore + surf, no swimming out), east = the Ferrum river then the rock wall. The big river runs north-south from a mountain waterfall to the sea and splits the near side (east, levels 1-5) from the far side (west, levels 5-10). The river is not a hard wall (owner 2026-10-09): every river cell is wadeable but slow (proposal: 40 % walk speed, no attacks while wading, current pushes south), with two shallow fords (F, 70 % speed); the far side is held by stronger beasts, the gate is gear tier. Bridge A (south, open) leads to the open fields; bridge B (north, broken, repaired by the level-5 quest = manager lean, owner Q1) leads straight to the foothills.
