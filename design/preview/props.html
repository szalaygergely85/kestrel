Loads ../palette.js and ../models/*.js. Every animation is shown at near (2x, nearest sampling, as the engine enlarges up close), mid (1x) and far (the hand-drawn half LOD), plus the full frame strip. Lit cells use util.shadeSprite; emissive cells (flames, embers, glints) ignore light and fog. The background is dark by default; tick "lit stone backdrop" to test readability against a torch-lit wall.
Scene grid (D-009): a sprite at a given distance covers rows/60 times more cells on a finer grid, so the engine samples the same art at 1.5x (240x90) or 2x (320x120) and each cell is drawn 1.5x / 2x smaller. The near / mid / far cards follow that: at 320x120 "mid" is the art at 2x nearest sampling in 6 px cells. Same size on screen, blockier glyph texture.
ART-OWN-001 (readability rework): the section "in-game size" below draws every prop the way the engine does (on-screen rows = world.h * planeDistY / distance, nearest sampling, half LOD below 0.75) at 160x60 and 240x90 side by side, in front of a lit stone wall and floor, at the distance the player really sees it from. Each pair shows the SAME piece of a 1920x1080 screen: left the 160x60 grid (12x18 px cells), right 240x90 (8x12 px cells), drawn at the zoom below.
