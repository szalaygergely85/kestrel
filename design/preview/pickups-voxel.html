Kestrel : voxel pickups, gear drops, potions, floor lamp (OB-132 / OB-137)

design/models/pickups_voxel.js. Every model turns (the game turns them with the entity yaw). Camera pitched down like first person looking at the ground. The renderer here is a small standalone voxel DDA with the real v1 surface shader (palette.util.shade, material ramps + emissive), no engine import, so it also opens from file://. "world scale" draws every model in the same 1.2 m frame (the sizes as they lie side by side in the game); "fit" fills each frame.
