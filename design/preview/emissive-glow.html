Design note: docs/architecture.md 38.12 (derived point lights + soft bleed pass + halo in edge), queue docs/sprints/sprint-8-queue.md "Emissive lighting". Every panel is a coloured-letter mockup computed by this page (a tiny cell ray caster on a crop of the 400 x 150 frame, 75 deg HFOV, cells twice as tall as wide, the real voxel data of questMark, chestSmall and the hook lantern, palette colours / materials / ramps). It is not an engine capture: no textures, no edge pass, no shadows, no LVIS occlusion. Columns: off = today (material self-emissive only; placed lights such as the hook lamp and the brazier torch stay on), subtle, medium, strong = derived lights + bleed + halo (the 38.12 High tier) at three strengths. Sliders edit the preset picked under "edit preset"; copy values emits the 38.12 keys. Nothing is applied: palette.js, detail-pass.js, models, levels and engine are unchanged.   Invented by this page (not in 38.12): lightGain (multiplier on the derived intensity = the "light intensity"
  slider), haloR (halo reach in cells on sky / far cells; 38.12 uses k <= 1), haloK (halo transfer
  a = 1 - exp(-haloK * sum)), bleedNorm: 'kernel' (the bleed sum is divided by the full kernel weight at
  the cell's R, so R changes the reach and not the strength), glowMin (38.12's literal 0.3 source threshold),
  materials[k].glowColor (glow / derived-light colour when the base colour is wrong for the glow, e.g. brass_glint white).




Findings (for the PO / architect)


    
  
  • Hue: brass_glint (the '!' core, the chest seam, the lamp glint) has base: 'white'. With 38.12 as written,
    the derived light and the bleed of the golden '!' are white. Toggle "glow hue" to compare. Proposal: an optional
    material field glowColor (palette colour key; default = base): brass_glint.glowColor = 'brassLight'
    (palette edit, owner OK needed; no silent asset change).
    • 
  
  • Hidden voxels count: the pack-time derive counts every voxel with emissive >= 0.5 in the model, including the
    sealed glint plus in the chest floor / lamp base and the chest loot pool (still lit after opened hides it).
    chestSmall: same light closed, open and opened. Suggest def overrides: chests and the hook lantern light: false
    (their fx / placed lights already follow the state), questMark either the formula or light: {preset} from
    questMarkFx.light. Compare with the "derived light" select.
    • 
  
  • Halo reach: the '!' core line sits 2 voxels inside the gold bevel + 1 voxel dark rim, so it is >= 3 cells from the
    sky at 12 m (~13 cells at 3 m). With 38.12's k <= 1 kind-0 kernel the '!' gets no halo at all. The mock
    uses the bleed kernel shape up to haloR cells (<= R_MAX) on kind-0 cells; haloR 1 reproduces 38.12.
    • 
  
  • Bleed range: R = clamp(R_m * planeDistY / dist, 1, R_MAX) is always clamped to R_MAX here (planeDistY ~130 rows/m:
    R_m 1.2 m = 52 cells at 3 m). 4 cells = ~5 cm at 3 m, ~20 cm at 12 m: the bleed is a soft rim on the glowing object and its
    contact surfaces; the pool of light on the ground / wall comes from the derived point light.
    • 
  
  • Sprites: the lamp flame and the brazier fire are sprites (drawn after edge), so they cannot feed bleed / halo (38.12).
    Only emissive voxels glow. The wick / coal-bed proposals (existing ember_core / ember_glow materials,
    toggles above) show what the lamp and brazier would gain; without them the lamp and brazier columns differ only by derived
    lights from the sealed lamp glint.
