Kestrel : voxel props (D-019, US-056) : lever, lamp, boulder, rubble, canvas heap, gondola, strut, envelope heap, relay

Solid props as real 3D voxel models (architecture.md 15.1 format, 15.3 content contract), Blood-style: fixed in the world, bright rim over a dark body, dark contour. Data: design/models/voxel_props.js (batch 1: lever, lantern) and design/models/voxel_tower.js (batch 2: the rest), materials ASSETS.voxelMaterials (proposed, merge pending). Rendering uses the engine oracle itself: packVoxelModel, computeVoxelPose and marchVoxelRay are imported from engine/voxel/, so parts, pivots, pose sampling and the DDA hit are the engine's. The page adds only the camera, a directional / point light, the v1 reference surface shader (palette.util.shade, fk = 1 for every face as 15.2 item 5) and an approximate edge pass (cap / side / lip against farther cells, then edges.modelRim x fg and bg on rule cells). Flames and the knob glint are drawn as small camera-facing billboards with a depth test (they are separate billboard props in the game); the relay glow is not drawn (US-022). Needs http (ES modules): python -m http.server from the repo root, open /design/preview/voxel-props.html.

In-game size: 160x60 and 240x90 (engine projection, 75 deg HFOV, 16:9)

The same 1920x1080 screen area at both grids: left a 60x30 crop of the 160x60 grid (12x18 px cells), right a 90x45 crop of 240x90 (8x12 px cells). Rays are the sector caster's (d = dir + plane*cameraX, z = (horizonRow - row - 0.5) / planeDistY, t = perpendicular distance), pitched to look at the prop. Each model has its typical in-game view (distance, eye height above the prop's floor, floor + backdrop material from tower.js); the slider scales the distance. Lever: on the ledge floor, backdrop selectable. Lamp: on the step-8 wall face. Envelope heap: seen from above (the summit breach), no backdrop.
