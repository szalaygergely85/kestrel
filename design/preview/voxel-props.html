Kestrel : voxel props (D-019) : lever and brass lamp on its bracket

Solid props as real 3D voxel models (architecture.md 15.1 format, 15.3 content contract), Blood-style: fixed in the world, bright rim over a dark body, dark contour. Data: design/models/voxel_props.js (ASSETS.voxelModels, proposed materials ASSETS.voxelMaterials). Rendering uses the engine oracle itself: packVoxelModel, computeVoxelPose and marchVoxelRay are imported from engine/voxel/, so parts, pivots, pose sampling and the DDA hit are the engine's. The page adds only the camera, a directional / point light, the v1 reference surface shader (palette.util.shade, fk = 1 for every face as 15.2 item 5) and an approximate edge pass (cap / side / lip against farther cells, then edges.modelRim x fg and bg on rule cells). Flames and the knob glint are drawn as small camera-facing billboards with a depth test (they are separate billboard props in the game). Needs http (ES modules): python -m http.server from the repo root, open /design/preview/voxel-props.html.
